# Wedding invitation keepsake

## What I’ll build
- A full-screen opening card with drifting botanical petals, the couple’s names, date, invitation line, and a smooth “Open” reveal.
- One long, mobile-first invitation styled like tactile English countryside stationery, centered as a narrow paper sheet on larger screens.
- A taped cover portrait, ceremony details, staggered editorial gallery, reception details, live countdown, October 2026 calendar, directions, RSVP sheet, and persistent guestbook.
- A compact floating audio control that starts after opening and supports a visitor-selected audio file, with an original gentle instrumental fallback.

## Visual treatment
- Ivory paper, warm charcoal, taupe ink, paper grain, subtle shadows, masking tape, a wax seal, and restrained dried botanical linework.
- Editorial serif typography paired with understated labels and metadata.
- Soft opacity and transform reveals, gentle petal motion, and reduced-motion support.

## Technical details
- Keep everything on the existing home page using React and Tailwind CSS.
- Generate a cohesive set of couple and countryside wedding photographs for the invitation.
- Store guestbook entries locally in the visitor’s browser.
- Build Google Calendar and Google Maps links without external services.
- Validate the final page at mobile and desktop sizes, including interactions, scrolling, audio controls, and overflow.
